He hits the river. The impact stings, but not nearly as much as what comes next. There's blood all around him, unexpectedly opaque and plunging his world into darkness.

Being dead... isn't hard. It's the dying part that hurts.

A sudden gut-wrenching pain that rises up his throat causes Ryoji to sharply gasp, ... hurts ...he chokes ... hurts ...sinks ... and still ...hurts ...breathe ... still alive. His lungs struggle for air that is far away from this place.

Nothing but blood.

His heart beats steady.

His heart beats steady.

His heart beats steady.

How long   ...?

He should be dead. ......     Not that easy.

Ryoji ...hurts ...is surrounded by darkness ...cannot move Thanatos . can move. ... ...total darkness,   so ? . Something familiar calls for his attention, and Ryoji listens. The sound... hurts. Ryoji is drawn to it. ...   singing... ... Summoning . hurts. Thanatos . (hurts) is as easy as breathing. It's never been harder, but Ryoji has somewhere else to be.

...

 

Thanatos breaks the surface with an effort it shouldn't need, and Ryoji's human body is dragged out a moment later. It immediately starts convulsing. Only small, choked sounds at first, then when enough blood has spilled out of its mouth for air to replace it, Ryoji starts coughing in earnest, his breath stuttering and wheezing.

Fighting to focus through the pain, Ryoji makes absolutely sure it lands on solid ground before he unsummons Thanatos. It's lucky Thanatos has separate senses, because Ryoji's human senses are all fucked up. He can't see and everything sounds like it's underwater and he tastes blood.

Ryoji would have drowned if he could. That was his whole plan. He was counting on it.

By the time he finds his way out of the river, to the vague sound of singing, he's still alive. His heart beats steady. It never wavered once.

As soon as Thanatos' form stops holding him up, Ryoji crumples to the ground against the railing. On the road side this time, he made sure. His limbs aren't strong enough to move into a more comfortable position, and all Ryoji can do is turn his head so he doesn't keep choking. He spits out more blood.

He's absolutely drenched in it. It's thick and cloying in his throat and in his eyes and soaking his clothes.

There's a very stark feeling of distress constricting his lungs even once all the blood is out. It feels entirely foreign.

It takes several minutes for Ryoji's ragged gasps to give way to a somewhat-steady breathing pattern. He's still coughing every few seconds, but he's not throwing up blood anymore.
